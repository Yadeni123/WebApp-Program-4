A Doll's House

Act I - Scene 1

HELMER. [Calling from his room.] Is that my little skylark twittering out there?

NORA. [Opening some of the packages.] Yes, it is!

HELMER. Is it my little squirrel bustling about?

NORA. Yes!

HELMER. When did my squirrel come home?

NORA. Just now. [Puts the bag of macaroons into her pocket and wipes her mouth.] Come in, Torvald, and see what I have bought.

HELMER. Don't disturb me. [A little later, he opens the door and looks into the room, pen in hand.] Bought, did you say? All these things? Has my little spendthrift been wasting money again?
